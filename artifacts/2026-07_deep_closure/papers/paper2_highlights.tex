\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item The Quranic dawn criterion is formulated as a psychophysical detection problem
\item One calibrated constant reproduces 116 years of dawn observation at 0.26 deg median
\item A year of Birmingham panel dawns matched with zero retuning, 20 deg from calibration
\item Fixed angles miss by 1.6-4.4 deg RMS where defined; MWL 18 fails on 15 of 42 dates
\item False dawn, skyglow, clouds, and aerosol shift the times with stated uncertainty
\end{itemize}
