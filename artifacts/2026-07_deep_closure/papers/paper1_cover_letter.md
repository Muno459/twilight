# Cover letter, Paper 1 (methods)

To the Editors, Journal of Quantitative Spectroscopy and Radiative Transfer

Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "Absolute sky radiance through
deep twilight: a backward Monte Carlo model with three-dimensional cloud
transport, externally validated to solar zenith angle 103 degrees," for
publication in JQSRT.

The deep twilight regime, solar zenith angles beyond about 96 degrees, is
where plane-parallel and pseudospherical methods are structurally
inapplicable and where, to our knowledge, no published radiative transfer
comparison has previously reached under cloud. The manuscript presents an
open-source backward Monte Carlo model with spherical refracting geometry,
full Stokes polarization, and voxel-level three-dimensional cloud
transport, together with the variance-reduction stack that makes the
regime tractable, including a bidirectional connection estimator that
couples backward chains to light subpaths traced from the sunlit
terminator. The validation program spans reference codes (DISORT, MYSTIC,
SHDOM), published sky-brightness measurements, and a century of human dawn
observation; its deepest tier referees cloud decks at twilight geometry
against MYSTIC runs of a billion photons to solar zenith angle 103
degrees, with every cell of the tier statistically consistent with its
reference.

A companion manuscript, submitted alongside this one, applies the model to
the visual detection of dawn and dusk for religious timekeeping; the two
are written to stand alone but review naturally as a pair, and we would be
grateful if the editors could assign them together.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and all
results are reproducible from the public code repository referenced in the
text. The author declares no competing interests.

Sincerely,
Mostafa Mahdi
University of Copenhagen
